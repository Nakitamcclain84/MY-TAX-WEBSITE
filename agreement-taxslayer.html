5. BANK PRODUCTS, FEES AND PAYOUTS

Bank Products. {{SHORT_NAME}} will help ERO apply for bank products (such as refund transfers) through {{SHORT_NAME}}'s bank partner(s). Approval is decided by the bank, not {{SHORT_NAME}}, and is not guaranteed. ERO must pass the bank's compliance exam and any other bank requirements before offering bank products.

Fee Schedule. The per-return fees for returns filed with a bank product are listed in {{SHORT_NAME}}'s current Fee Schedule, attached as Schedule A. They are deducted from the client's refund through the bank product. They are not billed up front and do not apply to returns filed without a bank product.

Required and Optional Fees. The {{SHORT_NAME}} service bureau fee and the transmitter fee are REQUIRED on every return filed with a bank product. Audit protection and identity theft protection are OPTIONAL products. They are charged only on returns where the client chooses to buy them, and ERO must not add them to a return unless the client has agreed.

Fee Changes. If a bank, software company or other third party changes its fees, the new fees apply automatically when they take effect, without an amendment to this Agreement. {{SHORT_NAME}} will update the Fee Schedule and notify ERO of any change to {{SHORT_NAME}}'s own fees before it applies.

Add-On Fees. ERO may add its own add-on fees to bank-product returns, up to the limits set by the bank and software company.

Add-On Payout Timing. Add-on fees are not paid when a return funds. The bank or software company releases them to {{SHORT_NAME}} in batches, and {{SHORT_NAME}} pays ERO within ten (10) business days after {{SHORT_NAME}} receives them. Expected timing: add-on fees on returns funded January 1 through April 15 are paid after June 15, and add-on fees on returns funded April 16 through October 15 are paid after December 15, unless the bank or software company releases them sooner. ERO SHOULD NOT RELY ON ADD-ON FEES FOR CASH FLOW DURING THE FILING SEASON. ERO's own prep fees are paid by the bank under the bank's terms and are not held by {{SHORT_NAME}}.

Fee Disclosure. ERO must disclose all fees charged to the client, including prep fees, add-on fees and bank product fees, as required by the bank and applicable law. ERO will not charge any fee the bank or law does not allow.

Funding Ratio. ERO must keep a funding ratio of at least 80% on bank-product returns. The funding ratio is the number of ERO's bank-product returns that fund, divided by the number of ERO's bank-product returns the IRS accepts, measured for the current season to date. For example, if the IRS accepts 100 of ERO's bank-product returns and 85 of them fund, ERO's funding ratio is 85%. The bank calculates the ratio using its own reports, and the bank's calculation controls.

If the Funding Ratio Drops. A ratio below 80% at one point in the season does not automatically end ERO's bank products. If {{SHORT_NAME}} learns ERO's ratio has dropped below 80%, {{SHORT_NAME}} will notify ERO and help review the cause (for example, returns rejected, offset or filed with errors). The bank alone decides whether to warn, limit or remove ERO's bank products. If the bank removes them, {{SHORT_NAME}} is not liable and cannot override the bank, and ERO may not submit bank products through a different bank during the Term.

Reconciliation. {{SHORT_NAME}}'s records and the bank's funding reports control. If a fee is reversed, charged back or not funded, {{SHORT_NAME}} may adjust amounts owed to ERO accordingly.

6. ERO RESPONSIBILITIES

Credentials. ERO (and every preparer ERO allows to use the software) must hold a valid, current PTIN before preparing any return.

Compliance. ERO will follow all IRS rules that apply to paid preparers and EROs, including IRS Publication 1345, Circular 230 where it applies, and due diligence requirements (Form 8867) for EITC, CTC/ACTC/ODC, AOTC and head of household claims.

Client Data. ERO will protect client information as required by law, including the FTC Safeguards Rule and IRS Publication 4557, keep a written information security plan (WISP), and get client consent under IRC Section 7216 before using or sharing client information for any purpose other than preparing the return.

No Fraud. ERO will not file false or unsupported returns, inflate credits or deductions, use a client's refund for anything the client did not authorize, or charge fees based on the size of the refund.

Records. ERO will keep copies of returns, client documents, due diligence records and signed consents for at least three (3) years, and share them with {{SHORT_NAME}} on request if needed to answer a bank, IRS or software company inquiry.

Account Security. ERO will not share logins, and is responsible for everything done under its accounts.

Registration. ERO will send {{SHORT_NAME}} the documents needed to register its software and bank products, including its EFIN summary and ERO ID, and the same documents for each sub-office or location it adds.

No Reselling. ERO will not resell, sublicense, copy or share the software, {{SHORT_NAME}}'s training or {{SHORT_NAME}}'s materials, except with its own preparers as this Agreement allows. Each office that transmits returns needs its own license.

7. ERO BACKEND BONUS

Bonus. ERO can earn a backend bonus on each of its team's funded bank-product returns during the season, at the following levels. Each rate applies only to the returns within that level:

(a) Returns 1 to 50: $2 per return

(b) Returns 51 to 150: $5 per return

(c) Returns 151 to 300: $7 per return

(d) Returns 301 and up: $10 per return

Example. With 200 funded returns, the bonus is (50 x $2) + (100 x $5) + (50 x $7) = $100 + $500 + $350 = $950. It is NOT 200 x $7. Reaching a higher level raises the rate only for the returns within that level, not for returns already counted at lower levels.

Counting Returns. A return counts only when it was filed with a bank product, the bank product funded, and {{SHORT_NAME}} has verified the funding on the bank's report. Returns must be logged in {{SHORT_NAME}}'s return tracker.

Payment. The bonus is paid after the tax filing season closes, once {{SHORT_NAME}} completes its reconciliation with the bank. {{SHORT_NAME}} may reduce the bonus for returns that are reversed, charged back, rejected or found to violate this Agreement. No bonus is earned on {{SHORT_NAME}}'s own team or on returns filed in breach of this Agreement.
