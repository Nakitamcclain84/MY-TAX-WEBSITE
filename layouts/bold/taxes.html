Checklist

What to bring

  • Photo ID for you and your spouse
  • Social Security cards or ITIN letters for everyone on the return
  • W-2s, 1099s and any other income forms
  • Childcare costs and provider info
  • Form 1098-T for college students
  • Bank routing and account number for direct deposit
  • Last year's return, if you have it
