FAQ

Common questions

Is this the real TaxSlayer Pro and OLT Pro software?

Yes. You get the same professional software sold direct. We supply it as a service bureau and add setup, training and support.

Why is it cheaper than buying direct?

As a service bureau, part of our income comes from a small per-return fee on bank product returns, which lets us lower the up-front price.

Do I need an EFIN?

You need a PTIN to prepare returns. PTIN Partners can prepare under our EFIN. To run your own office with your own software, you need an active EFIN. We help you apply.

What's the difference between PTIN Partner and buying software?

PTIN Partners prepare under our EFIN and split fees with us, with no software cost. Buying software means you run your own office and keep your fees.

When do I get access?

After payment and your signed agreement, we schedule your setup session. Access opens when the vendor releases the new season's software.
